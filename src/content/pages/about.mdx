I'm a healthcare technology leader on Vancouver Island. My day job is data infrastructure and architecture at Island Health. The rest of the time I'm trying to make sense of how systems, people, and technology fit together, and writing down what I learn.

## About me

- **Current role:** Director, Data Infrastructure and Architecture at Island Health, leading data warehousing, data modernization, and AI support and compliance for the health authority.
- **Education:** Currently pursuing an MBA at Royal Roads University, specializing in International Business and Innovation Management.
- **Leadership:** Directed multi-disciplinary teams, developed strategies, and managed critical projects including pandemic response planning and digital transformation.
- **Volunteer work:** Treasurer and Director at Large for the Canadian College of Health Leaders – Vancouver Island Chapter, supporting mentorship and professional growth in the community.
- **Values:** Curiosity, growth, courage, respect, and compassion guide everything I do. I want to understand complex systems, challenge assumptions, and find insights worth sharing in technology, healthcare, and leadership.

## About the writing

The [writing](/writing/) here is called **Drift & Convergence**. It is about exploring the flow of ideas, systems, leadership, and making sense of complexity, in writing that stays approachable even when the tools and topics are not. It runs in two complementary series.

**Convergence** is the intentional practice of systems leadership—how we create alignment, navigate complexity, and lead change when the path forward isn't clear. It draws on what I've seen in healthcare transformation, cloud adoption, and organizational change: frameworks for making sense of complex systems, approaches for leading without formal authority, and reflections on creating the conditions where different perspectives can converge into action. It is for leaders working where people, technology, and organizations meet.

**Drift** is where curiosity leads—emerging technologies, AI capabilities, development practices, and the unexpected connections that surface when you follow interesting threads. It is the experimental space where I document hands-on learning, test new tools, and see what happens when you push a technology past its obvious uses, including the parts that didn't work. Expect technical deep dives, lessons from building real projects, and reflections on where technology is heading and what it means for how we work.
