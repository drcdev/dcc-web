**Drift & Convergence** is all about exploring the flow of ideas, systems, leadership, and making sense of complexity. Approachable content about useful tools and complex topics. I've created two complementary series to help share in a meaningful way.

**Convergence** explores the intentional practice of systems leadership—how we create alignment, navigate complexity, and lead change in environments where the path forward isn't clear. Drawing from healthcare transformation, cloud adoption, and organizational change initiatives, this series examines the mindset shifts and practical tools that help leaders build collective solutions across boundaries.

You'll find frameworks for making sense of complex systems, approaches for leading without formal authority, and insights on creating conditions where diverse perspectives converge into meaningful action. Whether you're facilitating large-scale change, designing critical systems, or building alignment across teams, Convergence offers guidance for leaders working at the intersection of people, technology, and organizational transformation.

**Drift** is where curiosity leads—an exploration of emerging technologies, AI capabilities, development practices, and the unexpected connections that surface when you follow interesting threads. This is the experimental space where I document hands-on learning, test new tools, and investigate what happens when you push technologies beyond their obvious applications.

From building AI integrations and understanding reliability challenges, to automating development workflows and discovering better ways to work, Drift captures the process of exploration itself. Expect technical deep-dives, lessons from building real projects, and reflections on where technology is heading and what it means for how we work and create.

## **About Me**

- **Current Role:** Cloud Architect at Island Health, designing scalable Microsoft 365, Power Platform, and integration solutions tailored to the healthcare sector.
- **Education:** Currently pursuing an MBA at Royal Roads University, with a specialization in International Business and Innovation Management.
- **Leadership:** Directed multi-disciplinary teams, developed comprehensive strategies, and managed critical projects like pandemic response planning and digital transformation.
- **Volunteer Work:** Treasurer and Director at Large for the Canadian College of Health Leaders – Vancouver Island Chapter, fostering mentorship and professional growth within the community.
- **Values**: I'm guided by curiosity, growth, courage, respect, and compassion in everything I do. My work is driven by a deep desire to understand complex systems, challenge assumptions, and uncover meaningful insights in technology, healthcare, and leadership.
