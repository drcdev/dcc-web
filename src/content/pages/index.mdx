I help healthcare and technology leaders make sense of complex technology change: teams adopting AI, organizations modernizing their systems, and leaders who need a clear plan they can act on.

## What I do

I give advice, run workshops, and speak at events. The work is practical: naming the real problem, choosing a sensible path, and getting a team moving on it.

The rest of this site is where I think out loud. The writing covers systems leadership and hands-on experiments with AI, and the project pages show what I've built along the way, including the parts that didn't go to plan.
