This policy replaces the former cookie policy. **Last updated:** 29 September 2026.

## Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies, including through its visitor statistics. The only thing kept in your browser is your theme choice (light or dark). It stays in local storage on your device and is never sent to this site.

## Visitor statistics

The main site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not track individuals. Preview versions of the site do not use it.

## Hosting

Cloudflare serves the pages of this site. To do that, Cloudflare processes standard request information, such as your IP address.

## The contact form

When the contact form is available, it collects only what you type into it. The fields it asks for are to be confirmed.

Submissions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. Where that database is located is to be confirmed. They are deleted automatically after a retention period. The retention period is to be confirmed.

## Spam protection

Sending the contact form may run a spam-protection check by a third-party service. The service receives technical information about your browser so it can tell people from automated submissions. The service and exactly what it receives are to be confirmed.

## Your choices

You can ask what this site holds about you, and you can ask for your contact submission to be deleted. How to make a request is to be confirmed.
