## Summary

The neutral (dusk) topic pill now carries `dark:border-dusk-400`, a 1px edge at 4.00:1 against the
dusk-800 card and 4.78:1 against the page, so its shape shows in dark mode. Fill, text and the
light theme are unchanged.
